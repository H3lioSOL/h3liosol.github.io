Lyric Kierata


I could only lead you so far. I believe in who you are.

— Fish in a Birdcage,
Rule #9 - Child of the Stars

Lyric Kierata was a Prey Terrestrial, specifically an Antelope & Goat. He was of a nomadic group of Prey animals, away from the villages, with his younger brother, girlfriend,and his daughter. He planned to have another child, but he died before they were born. He was killed by poachers, passing his axe to his older child.

Biography

Lyric Kierata was born (Year 25), born a Nomadic Prey. He was stronger than others around him since his birth, while also being a quick learner and a competant leader. When Lyric was 7 years old, his younger brother, Axia, was born. Axia wasn't as strong as Lyric was when he was born, nor did he grow up to be. Lyric taught Axia to instead be intuitive, nimble, quick and observant. They were growing in a time where predators were increasingly hunting for horns, thick fur, unique kills. When Lyric was 12 years old, his father was killed by predators for those exact reasons, leaving him to take care of his family with any help his mother could give. He put extra care into teaching his brother to survive above all else.

When he was 15 years old, Lyric met a girl, a girl who had lost her mother to the same type of predators who took his father from him. He became friends with her and eventually cared for her as if she was part of his own family. Her family ended up joining his in a sense, as they began to travel together. By 17 years old, Lyric had become romantically involved with the girl. When he was 20 and she was 19, they'd ended up having a daughter together. He taught her everything he'd taught Axia and more, since he'd learned much more in the time since he was a child. Two years later, his girlfriend got pregnant again with a boy. Not long before the boy was born, Lyric was caught by poachers and killed for his horns. When he died, his mother's axe was passed from him to his daughter.

Extra

Acts as a father figure toward his younger brother, since they lost their father when Axia was 5. He had to take responsibility so his brother would be safe. Tends to be seen as a leader figure by whatever group he's with at the time, so he takes care of them provides. Had a daughter with his partner of 7 years and planned on having another child, but died before they were born.

Owns the axe that's been passed down through his mother's side of their family, and passed it to his daughter as well. He gave his father's dagger to Axia. Was taught a lot about survival by their father and learned the rest on their own after he died. They taught all they could to Axia, too. Moves around a lot with Axia, both by preference and necessity.
